Vessel No. 2
Less stuff. More real.

My ceramic art is built around the belief that the home should be free of excess and the things you have should matter. It's not about minimalism — it's about connection. And if you don't connect, why do you need it?

My goal is for you to build a relationship with a piece over time, to commune with it and communicate with it. To feel its presence in the room, in your hands, at your table.

Functional, functionless, on a table, on a shelf — my work aims to share a bit of itself with you.

Most importantly, my work attempts to bring a feeling of Jewishness into the home — to bring the comfort and warmth of our heritage into your space — to surround itself with your family.
